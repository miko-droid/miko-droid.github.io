# Website Copy — v2 (revised structure)

This version reflects everything discussed:

- **The site's job:** somewhere to send people that isn't Instagram. A personal showcase, not a sales funnel — so people/portrait work belongs in the portfolio.
- **Footprint:** Australia-wide, anchored to the Scenic Rim. The copy keeps the anchor (that's what makes it yours) and adds the range.
- **Collections re-jigged to five:** Rainforest, Dawn & Dusk, Coast, Black & White, People. Waterfalls live inside Rainforest. "On Film" is retired as a collection — film photos slot into whichever collection fits, and film becomes a texture across the site rather than a bucket.
- **The differentiator:** context and voice. Instagram strips context; this site adds it. Two mechanisms: a one-line intro on each collection page, and an optional one-line story under individual photos. Both are pure copy — no build complexity.

As before: **keep** means the existing wording is already the right call.

## 5. Photo captions

Each caption now has an optional fourth field: **Story** — one dry sentence in your voice (the walk in, the leech count, why you went back four times). Not every photo needs one; the best ratio is maybe one in three. Where a photo has nothing to say, leave it blank and the caption just shows title, location, year as before.

**Locations must come from you** — a real, specific place name every time (`BINNA BURRA`, not `SCENIC RIM`). Specific beats general in every caption.

**Titles rule of thumb:** name a thing in the frame or a condition it was shot in, never a feeling. "Moss and fog" is a good title; "Serenity" is not.
