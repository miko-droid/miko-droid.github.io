## 5. Photo titles & captions

- **Titles:** keep all current titles. They're concrete, they came from the moment, and you're happy with the file names they map to. All "New title" cells left blank deliberately.
- **Locations:** still yours to fill, and worth doing before anything else on this list. `Lamington National Park` is good; where a photo has a more specific home (Binna Burra, O'Reilly's, a named falls), more specific is better. For the German photos, the town or region name is plenty.
- **Years:** five `?` entries to supply (Mountain Pools, Beech in fog, Trapped in the sky, Requiem, House on stilts, plus the three film frames in Film & Faces).
- **Stories:** now that the tone is sincere rather than wry, these get easier to write. One plain sentence about the day: "Third visit before the fog cooperated." "Shot on the walk out, nearly missed it." Aim for maybe one in three photos, and only where there's genuinely something to say. Blank is better than forced.

**The two Film & Faces questions are yours to answer, but for what it's worth:**
1. `05-afternoon-rest` — only you can judge whether it belongs on a public site. The test I'd apply: would you be comfortable with a stranger, a colleague or a wedding enquiry seeing it? If there's any hesitation, park it.
2. `20-the-car-park` — a photo that's neither a landscape nor a face, in a four-photo collection, will read as a stray. Either it has a story that earns its place (in which case give it that story line), or park it in holding until the collection is bigger and can absorb an odd one out.
