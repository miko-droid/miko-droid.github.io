# Website copy — v4 (2026-07-31)

What actually shipped this round. The earlier `SITE-COPY*.md` files are history
only; three of the four describe collections that no longer exist, and even
`v3-filled` drifted from the live site. **This one records the live state.**

## The diagnosis

The v3 voice ("warm and conversational, like showing a friend around") was
right and is unchanged. What read as cringe was a narrower thing: lines that
**rated the work** or **told the viewer how to feel**. Warmth was never the
problem; self-assessment was.

The rule going forward, on top of the `purpose/PURPOSE.md` voice guide:

> No line on the site is allowed to tell the visitor that a photograph is good,
> or how they should feel about it. Say where it is, what's in it, or what the
> day was like, and stop.

Still in force from v3: **no em dashes anywhere** (hyphen, `·` or semicolon),
first person, sincere, Australian, smileys where Michael wrote them.

## Home page

The hero is now **wordless**. Previously it carried:

- headline `Michael Robinson Landscape Photography` — which repeated the
  `MICHAEL ROBINSON` wordmark sitting directly above it
- subline `CANOPY · DAWN & DUSK · WATER · FORM & TEXTURE · FILM & FACES` — which
  listed the same five collections as the section directly below it

Both are gone. The page opens on a full-bleed photograph with the `SCROLL` cue
and nothing else, then the collection list. An `sr-only` `<h1>` carries the
document outline for screen readers and search results.

Everything else on the page (`COLLECTIONS`, the counts, the collection notes)
is unchanged.

What each cut removed: a self-rating clause (Form & Texture, Dawn & Dusk), a
composed aphorism (Water), sentimentality (Canopy), a precious flourish
(Film & Faces). Every concrete noun and real place name survived. The lowercase
`i've` typo went with the clause it lived in.

**Collection notes** (home page) are unchanged except Form & Texture, which lost
its trailing full stop — every other note is unpunctuated, and the meta
description template appends its own, which was producing `surface..`.

## About page

Three cuts, nothing else. Paragraphs 1 and 4 are untouched.

- **Para 2** ended "…the photos are my way of bringing a bit of it back -
  hopefully some of it lands for you too." Now ends "…and the photos are what I
  bring back." (The old ending instructed the reader's reaction.)
- **Para 3** was "Everything on the site is a keeper - the ones I'd print and
  hang at home." Now "The ones on here are the ones I'd print and hang at home."
  (Same criterion, without the boast.)
- **Para 5** named Instagram in the prose and again in the contact list two
  lines below. The prose mention is gone; the `INSTAGRAM` row stays.

## Unchanged, deliberately

Collection names and URLs, all 52 photo titles, the footer
(`ALL PHOTOGRAPHS © MICHAEL ROBINSON · DO NOT USE WITHOUT PERMISSION`), the
prints line and its smiley, the tagline, both meta descriptions, and every piece
of micro-copy (`SCROLL`, `COLLECTIONS`, `← ALL COLLECTIONS`, the roman numerals,
the counts, `EMAIL` / `INSTAGRAM` / `PRINTS`, the lightbox counter).
