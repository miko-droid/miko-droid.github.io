ABOUT

Hi, I'm Michael. I photograph landscapes around South East Queensland - the rainforest, the ranges, the coast - and wherever else the car ends up.

Being out there is honestly the point. Fog moving through the beech forest, first light on big country, a beach before anyone else is on it. The camera gives me a reason to be in those places at those hours, and the photos are what I bring back.

I keep going back to the same tracks around the Scenic Rim in different weather and different light; the rest come from travelling around. The ones on here are the ones I'd print and hang at home.

I also photograph people. Portraits, the odd wedding, friends - often on 35mm film, because it makes me slow down.

Thanks for stopping by. If you'd like a print, or just want to talk photos, my email's below.
